EFL Reward Bank

Save It or Spend It

Your EFL Credits stay banked until you choose to redeem them. Bigger rewards require more patience.

Redemption Rule: A franchise may claim one Legacy reward per league year. Spent EFL Credits are deducted from the owner's available balance, but lifetime Legacy Points and rank remain untouched.
