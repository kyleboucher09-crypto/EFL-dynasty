How the model works: Sleeper supplies the current EFL rosters, league scoring settings, player metadata and 2026 season projections. The model optimizes each roster into the strongest legal starting lineup, scores the next usable bench pieces for depth, penalizes current injury designations, and applies a position-weighted 2026 strength-of-schedule adjustment. The schedule component uses opponent fantasy points allowed by position from the 2026 LineupBeat schedule table (updated Aug. 22, 2026). These are forward-looking power rankings, not dynasty trade-value rankings.
