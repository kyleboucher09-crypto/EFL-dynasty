Case Bulletin 004

The Case Begins... Again

The current public explanation for why CASE 12-25 was reopened.

Why the case is open

During the 2025 Christmas Incident, no participant correctly identified the Grinch during the final vote. Under the terms of the Traitor Contract, that result activated Sandie's alternate victory condition.

Only after Sandie's win had already been declared did the hosts reveal Savannah as the pre-designated Grinch identity attached to the original game ending.

The distinction matters. A post-game reveal is not the same thing as the family successfully identifying the Grinch.

CASE 12-25 is now examining whether the 2025 reveal represented the true Grinch — or whether Savannah became the identity attached to an ending that was already compromised.

Review the record. Remember what actually happened. Trust carefully.

Exhibit 00 • Archived 2025Original Traitor Contract

How Sandie became the Traitor

During last year's investigation, Sandie discovered a concealed note that presented two choices.

Option 1: destroy the note, return the bait and continue normally.

Option 2: sign the Traitor Contract, secretly side with the Grinch and surrender the normal route to victory.

Sandie chose Option 2. If no participant correctly identified the Grinch by the final vote, Sandie would win. No one did. The contract triggered.

Important: Savannah was revealed by the hosts only after Sandie's victory had already been declared. The family did not identify Savannah through the final vote. CASE 12-25 is now investigating whether that post-game reveal ever represented the true Grinch at all.
