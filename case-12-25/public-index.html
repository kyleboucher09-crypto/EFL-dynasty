Case Bulletin 004

Why the Case Was Reopened

The public record now turns on one distinction: what the family identified, and what was disclosed after the game.

The contradiction

During the 2025 Christmas Incident, no participant correctly identified the Grinch during the final vote. Under the terms of the Traitor Contract, that result activated Sandie's alternate victory condition.

Only after Sandie's victory had already been declared did the hosts reveal Savannah as the Grinch identity attached to the original ending.

The distinction matters. A post-game disclosure is not the same as a successful identification by the family.

CASE 12-25 is now examining whether the 2025 disclosure established the true Grinch — or whether the real Grinch escaped unidentified.

How Sandie became the Traitor

Sandie discovered a concealed note offering two choices: destroy it and continue normally, or sign the Traitor Contract and accept an alternate route to victory.
